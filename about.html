About Pleasers Online

Pleasers Online is an entertainment resource for people planning private parties, bachelorette celebrations, birthdays and special occasions throughout Toronto and Southern Ontario.

Our goal is to help visitors discover independent entertainers, private dancers and creative party ideas throughout Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Hamilton and surrounding communities.

We encourage responsible celebrations, direct communication with entertainers and clear booking arrangements.
