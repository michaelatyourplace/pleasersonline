Booking Entertainment for Your Bachelorette Party

Before contacting an entertainer, make sure your group has agreed on the basic details of the celebration. Providing accurate information can make the booking process much easier.

  • Party Date: Confirm your preferred date and performance time.
  • Event Location: Provide the city and type of venue.
  • Group Size: Estimate the number of guests attending.
  • Entertainment Preferences: Discuss the performance style that suits the group.
  • Budget: Confirm the total price and any additional fees.
  • Venue Permission: Make sure outside entertainment is permitted.
  • Photography: Confirm whether photos and videos are allowed.

Always respect the performer's agreed boundaries and make sure everyone attending is comfortable with the planned entertainment.
