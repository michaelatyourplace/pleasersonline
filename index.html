What to Know Before Booking an Entertainer

Planning private entertainment is easier when you have the basic event details prepared before contacting a performer.

  • Event Date: Confirm the date and preferred performance time.
  • Location: Provide the city or neighbourhood where your celebration is taking place.
  • Group Size: Let the entertainer know how many guests are expected.
  • Entertainment Type: Discuss the type of performance that best suits your celebration.
  • Pricing: Confirm the total cost, travel expenses and any deposit requirements.
  • Venue Rules: Make sure your property or event venue permits outside entertainment.
  • Photography: Ask the entertainer before taking photographs or recording performances.
  • Respect: Follow agreed performance boundaries and respect the comfort of all guests.
